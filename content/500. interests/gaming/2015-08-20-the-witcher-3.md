Retro edit: The Witcher 3 is one of the greatest open-world RPGs of all time. In the early- and mid-2020s, with the likes of Sekiro and Ghost of Tsushima about, Witcher 3's combat system is showing its age, but everything else is still peak. *There are no fetch quests!* *There are no radiant quests!* Every side quest, not matter how small, has it's own unique bit of story or lore. This game also has two of the best DLCs of all time.

## The Highs

* The story.
* The world and environment.
* The side quests.
* No fetch quests.

## The Sighs

* The combat has become a bit weak, compared to more contemporary titles.

## (Still) Worth It?

Absolutely.
