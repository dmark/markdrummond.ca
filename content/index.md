I am an IAM Architect and identity security leader with deep experience designing and modernizing enterprise and consumer identity platforms in highly regulated environments. My work centres on building secure, scalable, and standards-based identity architectures that enable digital trust, Zero Trust security models, and modern cloud-native services — and on turning that architecture into identity programs the business can actually run on.

As Director of Digital Trust and Identity at a federally regulated Canadian financial services organization, I am building the organization's joint workforce and customer IAM (CIAM) program from the ground up, having inherited a domain with no formal program in either discipline. I own workforce IAM and CIAM architecture end-to-end — identity strategy, target-state architecture, governance, and platform modernization — but a defining part of the role is working directly with the business: partnering with product, digital, and customer experience stakeholders to shape CIAM strategy, authentication and onboarding experiences, and customer-facing digital trust, alongside the internal security and compliance mandate.

I lead and am growing a multidisciplinary IAM team spanning software engineering, IAM systems engineering, IAM operations and governance, developing team members' identity-specific expertise as the program matures, while staying hands-on in architecture and complex technical decision-making. My scope further includes passkey adoption, identity governance (IGA) capability development, cloud identity services, and consolidation of complex legacy environments onto modern identity platforms.

With nearly 30 years in technology, my background spans systems engineering, large-scale infrastructure modernization, cloud and SaaS operations, solution architecture, and identity security. Earlier in my career, I delivered pre- and post-sales identity architecture and implementation services at STANTIVE Technologies (a Sun Microsystems IMO), designing directory, identity, and messaging solutions for large public- and private-sector organizations.

I am actively involved in the identity community through IDPro, the Digital Governance Standards Institute, and the OpenID Foundation. I hold CISSP and CIDPRO® certifications and have contributed to the IDPro Body of Knowledge. My current focus is helping organizations align business objectives, security requirements, and emerging identity standards to deliver resilient, enterprise-scale identity solutions.
